Drag a knob up or down, scroll on it, or use the arrow keys. Double-click resets it. The menu under each control lets a right-hand movement play it, and it turns green while your hand drives it. Pedal ring: giving it a hand holds the sustain pedal down the whole time, and the hand sets how long notes ring. Bend: try Tilt, where an upright hand is in tune and tilting either way bends; it springs back when your hand leaves the frame.

MIDI out

Send what you play to another synth, a DAW or a hardware instrument: notes, the sustain pedal, knob moves as controllers and the tempo clock. Or record it straight to a .mid file.

Off

Records notes, pedal and knob moves. No MIDI device needed.

On Windows, to play into a DAW on this computer, make a virtual port with loopMIDI and pick it under Send to.

    About & support

    Made with permission from Eric Wei, the creator of Gesture Synth.

    Synth Conductor is free. No account, no ads, no tracking. The camera picture never leaves your computer: hand tracking runs inside the browser, and your settings are kept in this browser only.

    If it’s useful to you, you can chip in toward its development. Entirely optional; nothing is locked.

    Upright: normal chords. Lean right: flipped (with Pure maj/min: major / minor)

    Lean right: normal (major). Lean left: flipped (minor). Upright keeps your last choice
    left ↔ right

    Root in the bass (Height inversions on)
    top of screen

    bottom of screen

    Flat ♭ chord, e.g. ♭VII (Palm roll; can be set to sharp or off)
    fist / away

    Right hand · how it sounds

    0–4 fingers
    The five voicings you assigned (thumb not counted)
    thumb out
    Major 7th on every chord (thumb in removes it)
    left ↔ right, height, lean, distance, palm roll
    Any knob or fader whose hand menu is set to it. Defaults: cutoff, volume, color switch, reverb
    color switch, thumb out
    Flat 7th: IVmaj7 → IV7
    twist left / right, thumb out
    maj7 / 7 (7th type: Twist). Upright gives maj7 on major chords, m7 on minor
    color switch, thumb in
    Add a 9th: IV → IVadd9

    The Eric Wei hand setup is an homage to Eric Wei's Gesture Synth: lean your left hand right for major or left for minor, twist your right hand left for maj7 (m7 on minor chords) or right for 7 (°7 on minor chords), and use horns 🤘 / ILY 🤟 for 6 and 7.
